About

Who we are

The Cybersecurity & IT Student Association at Penn State Abington is dedicated to fostering a community of students passionate about technology, cybersecurity, and innovation. Our mission is to provide hands-on learning opportunities, professional development, and networking experiences that prepare members for success in the rapidly evolving fields of cybersecurity and information technology. Through collaboration, workshops, and real-world applications, we empower students to build technical skills, explore career pathways, and contribute to a safer, more connected digital world.

Biweekly hands-on session

Come Hack

Led by Moussa Toure. A recurring, hands-on cybersecurity meeting focused on learning how real systems are attacked, defended, investigated, and analyzed — guided learning plus practical exercises on cyber ranges and custom-built labs.

Offensive Security

  • Web application hacking
  • Linux & Windows system exploitation
  • Standalone target machines
  • Chained attack paths
  • Active Directory environments
  • Enterprise security
  • AI-assisted operations

Defensive & Analysis

  • Blue-team exercises
  • Security analysis
  • Phishing analysis
  • Log and artifact analysis
  • Incident investigation concepts
  • OSINT techniques
  • Detection and defensive thinking

Cyber ranges we use

TryHackMe OffSec Labs Hack Smarter PortSwigger + custom labs
Who: anyone interested in cybersecurity, offensive/defensive security, pentesting, security research, OSINT, or IT. No prior expertise required.
Bring: a laptop is recommended but not mandatory; workstations are available.

Ground rules: practice only against systems we own, systems provided for training, or systems we have permission to test — responsible skill-building, never unauthorized attacks.

First session: Thursday, September 17, 2026 · 12:15 – 1:15 PM · Woodland Building 132A (in person). Biweekly afterwards.
